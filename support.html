Common Questions

How do I delete my account? Go to Profile > Account Settings > Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all associated data, including pantry items, recipes, meal plans, and health/nutrition history.

How do I restore a Premium purchase? Go to Profile and tap "Restore Purchases" under the Premium section.

How do I turn off Apple Health / Google Health Connect syncing? Go to Settings within the app and toggle it off - this stops all further reading immediately.
