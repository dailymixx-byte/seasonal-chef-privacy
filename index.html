Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Information We Collect

Account information: when you create an account, we store your email address via Supabase Authentication.

Content you create: pantry items, shopping lists, meal plans, food ratings, dietary preferences, custom recipes, and your supplement list (name, dose, and timing of anything you choose to track) are stored under your account so they sync across your devices.

Nutrition and food logs: meals you log manually, by scanning a packaged food's barcode, by searching a food database, by photographing a meal, or by typing a short description of a meal are stored under your account, including the calories and macros associated with each entry.

Camera access: used to scan barcodes (pantry items, food logging, supplements) on-device, and to photograph a meal for AI-estimated macros. Barcode scans never leave your device as an image. A meal photo - or, if you use the "describe a meal" option instead, the text you type - is sent to our AI provider (see Third-Party Services) for analysis and is not stored by us afterward.

Microphone access: if you use hands-free voice commands in Cooking Mode, spoken commands are converted to text using your device's built-in speech recognition system. Depending on your device and settings, this may send a short audio clip over the network to Apple's or Google's speech recognition service to be transcribed - the same standard behavior as Siri or Google Assistant dictation elsewhere on your phone. We do not ourselves record, retain, or have access to this audio; it is used only to produce the text of your spoken command.

Health & Fitness Data

Body weight, body fat percentage, and body measurements (neck, shoulders, chest, arm, waist, abdomen, hip, thigh, calf) that you enter manually, that sync from a paired Bluetooth scale, or that you import from a CSV file you exported from another measurement app, are stored under your account and used to show your weigh-in history and trends, and to power features like adaptive calorie targets during a cutting phase and safe-rate-of-loss guidance.

Apple Health / Google Health Connect (optional, off by default): if you explicitly turn this on, we read your weight and body fat data (and, on iOS, waist circumference where available) from Apple Health or Google Health Connect so scales that sync there - even ones we don't otherwise support - work automatically. This is read-only: the app never writes anything back to Apple Health or Health Connect, and nothing is read from either until you turn the feature on in Settings. You can disconnect it at any time, which stops all further reading immediately.

Progress photos (optional): photos you choose to add are stored in a private location that only your account can access. They are never public, and are only ever viewed through short-lived, per-request links - not a permanent public URL.

All of the above is used only to show it back to you (history, trends, and the features listed above) and is never sold or shared with advertisers.
