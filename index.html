Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Information We Collect

Account information: when you create an account, we store your email address via Supabase Authentication.

Content you create: pantry items, shopping lists, meal plans, food ratings, dietary preferences, and any recipes you write yourself are stored under your account so they sync across your devices.

Camera access: if you use the barcode scanner to add pantry items, the camera is used only to read the barcode on-device - photos are not stored or uploaded.

How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to provide the app's features: syncing your pantry, shopping list, and meal plan across devices, and personalizing recipe search results to your dietary preferences and ratings. We do not sell your data.

Third-Party Services

Recipe search uses external recipe databases (Spoonacular, Edamam, TheMealDB, Tasty) and image services (Pexels) - search terms and ingredient names may be sent to these providers to return results. Nutrition lookups may use the FatSecret API. Your account credentials are never shared with these services.

Sign in with Apple / Google: if you use these options, the respective provider authenticates you and shares your email address with us; we never see or store your Apple or Google password.

Crash reports: if enabled, technical crash/error data (not your personal content) may be sent to Sentry to help us fix bugs.

Product analytics: if enabled, we send app usage data (which screens and features you use) to PostHog, associated with your account's user ID and email so we can, for example, respond to a support request about your account. This does not include your pantry contents, recipes, or other personal content you create.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database with row-level security, meaning your account can only access your own data. Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).

Your Choices

You can edit or delete your pantry items, shopping list, ratings, and custom recipes at any time from within the app. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from Profile > Account Settings.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to the app's support contact.
